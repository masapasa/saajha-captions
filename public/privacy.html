Privacy & imprint

Updated 30 October 2026. Saajha Captions is a free community pilot operated by Nubri GmbH.

Your recordings

Audio files are opened locally in your browser. Transcription and caption editing take place on your device. We provide no audio upload endpoint. We do not receive your audio, file name, draft captions, or downloaded captions. There is no account, advertising, or analytics tracking.

Audio and edits stay in tab memory and are discarded when you clear the recording or close the tab. Downloads remain wherever you save them. If you share or email them, the recipient receives those files. Use recordings you have permission to process, and avoid sharing sensitive personal material.

Downloads and local storage

The first transcription downloads a speech model and browser runtime from this website. Your browser can cache the app and these files for later use. Browsers can evict caches; test offline use before relying on it. Use “Remove downloaded files” in the app to remove its caches. Your interface language preference is stored locally. No recording content is saved in local storage or the app cache.

Hosting and contact

This website is hosted on Nubri’s shared Amazon Web Services server in the United States (US East, Northern Virginia). Loading the site or downloading model files sends ordinary connection metadata, including your IP address, to the host. This does not include the audio you open locally. Routine HTTP access logging is disabled for this pilot; limited infrastructure and security error logs may still contain connection metadata. Contact us to ask about personal data, request access or deletion of information you have sent us, or raise a privacy concern. You may also contact the competent data protection authority.

Contact: anu@nubri.co. Please do not send private recordings or other people’s identifying details in feedback.
