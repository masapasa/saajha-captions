Speech model & licenses

This pilot uses Whisper tiny multilingual, approximately 39 million parameters. The original model and weights are published by OpenAI under the MIT license. The ONNX conversion is distributed by onnx-community. This project does not claim authorship of Whisper or the ONNX conversion.

Model source: onnx-community/whisper-tiny. Pinned revision: ff4177021cc41f7db950912b73ea4fdf7d01d8e7. Encoder and merged decoder use 8-bit quantization. SHA-256 checksums are included in the downloadable source.

Inference uses Hugging Face Transformers.js (Apache-2.0) and Microsoft ONNX Runtime (MIT). Files are served by this site; transcription runs in your browser. There is no OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Llama, or other frontier model API call.

Accuracy and suitability

Small models can produce incorrect text or words that were never spoken. Performance varies by language, accent, recording quality and device. Nepali accuracy and low-end phone performance have not yet been established in community testing. Review every caption against the audio. This is a learning aid, not a verified transcript or professional advice.
